## 🌸 QUICKSTART MANUAL (How to Train with NatyaMudra)

### 1. Preparing Your Training Space
*   **Camera Placement:** Place your laptop, tablet, or phone on a stable surface at arm's length (about 1.5 to 3 feet / 0.5 to 1 meter away from you).
*   **Lighting:** Ensure your face and hands are clearly visible. Avoid bright background windows (backlighting) that turn your hands into silhouettes.
*   **Clothing/Background:** Solid backgrounds and long sleeves pushed back past your wrists allow the computer code to map your joints with optimal precision.

---

### 2. Granting Webcam Permissions
*   When launching **Practice Mode**, your browser will show a dialog prompt asking for access to your camera.
*   **Click "Allow."**
*   *Security Guarantee:* NatyaMudra analyzes video frames entirely in-memory on your computer’s processor. No video feed, image records, or live streaming are sent to external databases or exposed over the web.

---

### 3. Selecting Your Lineage (Dance School Mode)
*   At the top header dashboard, configure your preferred **dance style** from the dropdown menu (e.g., **Bharatnatyam**, **Kuchipudi**, **Kathakali**, or **Odissi**).
*   The research rules will adjust slightly to emphasize classical references and facts unique to your choice.

## 🎙️ GURU VOICE (GURUVANI) MULTILINGUAL SPEECH
One of NatyaMudra’s most powerful tools is the interactive **Guruvani** voice assistant. The coach reads description details and shāstra instructions as you focus your hand alignments.

### How to configure translation:
1.  Navigate to **Tutorial Mode** via the left side layout panel.
2.  Locate the **"Guru Voice"** configuration box next to the Mudra name.
3.  Choose your target language from the list, including:
    *   **Hindi (हिन्दी)**
    *   **Bengali (বাংলা)**
    *   **Tamil (தமிழ்)**
    *   **English (en-IN with Indian accentuation)**
    *   **Telugu, Malayalam, or Kannada**
4.  Click the gold **"Listen to Guruvani"** play button. The server-side generative processor will instantly translate the Sanskrit descriptions and guide you.
5.  *Tip:* Adjust your system speakers to comfortable listening levels.

## 🧘 MUDRA REFERENCE SHEET & ALIGNMENT STRATEGIES

Here are tips for achieving a 100% correctness rating in **Practice Mode**:

| Mudra Name | Meaning & Significance | How to Idealize Your Hand Alignment | Common Errors to Avoid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pataka (Flag)** | Streams, forests, clouds, and authority. | Fully open your hand. All 5 fingers must be strictly straight and pressed together vertically. Keep thumb flush next to your index. | Leaving gaps between fingers; bending knuckles slightly. |
| **Tripataka (3-Part Flag)** | Crown of kings, rising flames, or patterns. | Start in Pataka, then curl your **Ring finger** down towards your palm. Keep other fingers pointing high. | Dropping the pinky or letting other fingers bend. |
| **Kartarimukha (Scissors)** | Separation, lightning, and distance. | Index and Middle must extend straight, spreading apart in a sharp 'V' shape. Fold ring and pinky flat under thumb. | Holding index and middle closed together. |
| **Mayura (Peacock)** | The sacred peacock bird, cascading waters. | Bring the tip of your **Ring finger** to touch the tip of your **Thumb**. Hold Index, Middle, and Pinky upright. | Folding the index or ring fingers too loose. |
| **Alapadma (Water Lotus)** | Full blooming lotus flower. | Fan out all five fingers as wide as possible. Curve your hand backward slightly as if cupping holy water. | Holding fingers straight without the petal flare. |
| **Suchi (Needle)** | Pointing, the supreme soul, or number one. | Point index straight up. Fold middle, ring, and pinky flat into palm, locked secure under your thumb. | Dropping index sideways. |
| **Shikhara (Peak)** | Mountain peak, silent temple pillars. | Roll all four fingers tightly into a fist. Align your thumb to stand straight up like a beacon. | Tilting thumb sideways or loosening fingers. |
| **Kapittha (Elephant Apple)** | Goddess Lakshmi, picking flowers. | Start in Shikhara fist, then bend your **Index finger** over the tip of your upright thumb to form a hook. | Letting the index slide loose or thumb drop down. |

## 🛠️ TROUBLESHOOTING & FAQ

#### Q: The browser is stuck showing "Loading computer vision camera..."
*   **Solution:** Check if another browser tab or software (like Zoom or Teams) is currently occupying your webcam. Close competing applications, refresh the page, and select "Allow" when prompted again.

#### Q: Why is my scoring indicator fluctuating or low?
*   **Solution:** Ensure you are facing the camera directly. The geometric rule compiler is optimized for hand shapes presented flat-on or slightly angled to the sensor. High lighting contrast and a clear view of the wrist will stabilize scoring instantly.

#### Q: Does my progress save if I close this tab?
*   **Solution:** Yes! NatyaMudra saves all session high scores, daily streaks, levels, and badge unlocks in your browser's LocalStorage cache. It automatically attempts to mirror these with secure, encrypted clouds when connected to a Firebase database.
